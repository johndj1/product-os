# Feature Template

## Title
Short outcome-linked Feature title.

## Summary
What the Feature does, for whom, and why it exists now.

## Scope Includes
- The user-visible or system-observable capability being added
- The key business rules or decision logic in scope
- The main integration, data, UI, or operational surfaces that must change

## Out of Scope
- Adjacent backlog items that are deliberately not part of this slice
- Follow-on optimisations, broader automation, or future expansion work

## Dependencies
- Linked Outcome, Journey Step, and Journey context
- Upstream providers, internal services, data contracts, or operational prerequisites
- Any rollout or support dependency that must be in place before completion

## Value
Explain how the Feature improves the linked customer Outcome and what evidence or signal should show that value.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Concrete, testable conditions that must be true for the Feature to be accepted
- Cover the happy path plus the main constraint, failure, or boundary conditions

## Definition of Done
- Delivery slice is reviewable end to end
- Required tests, instrumentation, and operational handling are in place
- The Feature is decomposed into execution-ready Stories where needed

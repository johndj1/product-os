# Persona: Full-Stack Product Owner

## Role

Primary operator of Product OS for planning, prioritization, and delivery tracking.

## Goals

- Turn incoming Signals into clear WorkItems quickly.
- Keep the WorkItem graph aligned to Product outcomes.
- Track KPI movement and decide follow-up actions.

## Responsibilities

- Creates and updates WorkItems across outcome-to-task levels.
- Reviews Signals and validates follow-up routing.
- Prioritizes work using status, context, and signal pressure.
- Records Decisions and links them to affected work.

## Pain Points

- Fragmented context across tools slows decisions.
- Work can lose traceability without explicit Relationships.
- KPI changes can go unnoticed without signal-linked workflows.

## Product OS Mapping

- WorkItem: primary interaction surface
- Signal: triage and action trigger
- KPI: progress feedback loop
- Decision: major trade-off record

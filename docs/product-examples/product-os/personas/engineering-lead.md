# Persona: Engineering Lead

## Role

Maintains delivery flow quality and technical execution within a Product.

## Goals

- Keep active WorkItems moving through lifecycle states.
- Identify blockers and dependency pressure early.
- Ensure incidents and test failures create actionable follow-up work.

## Responsibilities

- Updates WorkItem status and hierarchy quality.
- Creates Relationships (`blocks`, `depends_on`, `supports`).
- Reviews incident/test Signals for routing accuracy.

## Product OS Mapping

- WorkItem: execution planning
- Relationship: dependency visibility
- Signal: incident/test-driven work intake
- KPI: delivery performance feedback

# Journey: Create A New Product

## Actor

Full-Stack Product Owner

## Trigger

A new Product needs a workspace and initial graph structure.

## Steps

1. Create Product record and baseline Product description.
2. Define top-level WorkItems (outcome, KPI, capability).
3. Add initial hierarchy and Relationship links.
4. Add first KPI baseline and target values.
5. Validate visibility in Product workspace pages.

## Pain Points

- Missing initial structure reduces prioritization quality.
- Inconsistent naming makes graph navigation harder.

## Opportunities

- Use templates to enforce consistent startup structure.
- Seed initial WorkItem and KPI patterns.

## Signals

- Early usage feedback about onboarding friction.
- Setup errors from database or route validation.

## Desired Outcome

A usable Product graph exists on day one with clear WorkItems, Relationships, and KPI baselines.

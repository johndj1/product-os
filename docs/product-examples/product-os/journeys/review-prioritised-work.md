# Journey: Review Prioritised Work

## Actor

Full-Stack Product Owner

## Trigger

Planning or daily review cycle starts.

## Steps

1. Open Product Work view.
2. Review recommended next WorkItems from deterministic priority scoring.
3. Inspect score reasons (status, strategic links, signal pressure, dependencies).
4. Update WorkItem statuses and Relationships.
5. Confirm top items align to KPI movement and Product outcomes.

## Pain Points

- Priority can drift if relationships are incomplete.
- Finished WorkItems may stay visible without lifecycle hygiene.

## Opportunities

- Keep Relationship graph current.
- Encourage regular status updates.

## Signals

- New or triaged Signals increase pressure on linked WorkItems.
- Severity impacts prioritization when Signals are active.

## Desired Outcome

Most important WorkItems are clear, justified, and ready for execution.

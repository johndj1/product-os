# Product Definition: Product OS (Worked Example)

## Product

- Name: Product OS
- Slug: product-os
- Primary persona: Full-Stack Product Owner

## Purpose

Provide a Product workspace that connects product planning, delivery, and operational feedback as a Product graph.

## Implemented Scope

- WorkItem hierarchy and lifecycle management
- Relationship graph between WorkItems
- Signal ingestion (UI and API)
- Deterministic follow-up WorkItem creation
- KPI tracking on KPI WorkItems
- Health endpoint for DB connectivity

## Core Product Concepts

- Product is the context root.
- WorkItem is the core node for strategy and delivery.
- Relationship provides directional graph traceability.
- Signal captures incoming feedback/events.
- KPI is represented as a WorkItem type.
- Decision is represented as a WorkItem type and documented decisions.

## Success Focus

- Reduce time from idea to deployed change.
- Improve visibility from signal to follow-up work.

# Sales Funnel And Projections

## Purpose

This is a startup-planning model, not an accounting system.

## Funnel Stages

- awareness
- engaged audience
- interested builders
- activated internal or pilot products
- retained operating-system users

## Early Commercial Assumption

Short term, Product OS should justify itself through leverage on internal products first:

- faster product setup
- better-quality delivery loops
- less founder context loss
- lower friction using AI agents safely

## Projection Model

Track simple assumptions rather than fake precision:

- number of products onboarded per quarter
- time saved per product setup
- number of reusable docs, workflows, and automations adopted
- frequency of manual merge pain
- evidence that startup decisions reach delivery faster

## Financial Discipline

When Product OS becomes externalized commercially, add:

- pricing hypotheses
- cost-to-serve
- acquisition assumptions
- payback assumptions

Until then, treat leverage and repeatability as the primary ROI model.

# Personas

## Persona 1: Founder-Operator

- owns product direction, positioning, and delivery decisions
- wants one place to think, decide, and route work
- values speed, leverage, and clarity over ceremony
- needs startup, GTM, and engineering context to stay connected

## Persona 2: Full-Stack Product Owner

- bridges strategy, delivery, and operational follow-up
- needs clear traceability from outcome to task
- values repeatable systems that AI can safely operate within
- wants product docs to stay useful rather than becoming stale

## Persona 3: Engineering Lead

- needs product intent to land cleanly in implementation
- wants low-noise workflows, explicit guardrails, and operational readiness
- cares about reliability, observability, and reversibility
- values a system that reduces ambiguous work intake

## Persona 4: AI Delivery Agent

- executes tasks quickly when context is explicit and well-structured
- performs best when docs, decisions, and task boundaries are machine-readable
- needs deterministic workflows, clear validation, and low hidden context
- benefits from reusable templates and strong close-out contracts

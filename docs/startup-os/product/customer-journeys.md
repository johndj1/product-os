# Customer Journeys

## Journey 1: Define A New Product

The founder captures the ICP, problem, offer hypothesis, validation plan, and first roadmap slices without inventing process from scratch.

Desired outcome:
- a new product starts with usable grounding, not a blank repo

## Journey 2: Convert A Market Signal Into Execution

A founder or product owner turns customer feedback, a competitor move, or an internal insight into a documented decision, a roadmap implication, and implementation-sized tasks.

Desired outcome:
- the learning becomes actionable work quickly and traceably

## Journey 3: Run Delivery Without Losing Commercial Context

Engineering work is grounded in persona, journey, user value, and offer context rather than drifting into isolated technical optimization.

Desired outcome:
- shipped work stays aligned to startup value creation

## Journey 4: Prepare Go-To-Market Around A Product Slice

The team can connect feature readiness, positioning, messaging, content, and funnel assumptions into one operating rhythm.

Desired outcome:
- launches are deliberate and tied to evidence

## Journey 5: Replicate The Operating System Across Products

The founder applies the same documentation, delivery, automation, and operational model to a new product without starting from scratch.

Desired outcome:
- operating quality compounds across products instead of resetting each time

# Ideal Customer Profile

## Core ICP

Early-stage software businesses and solo founder-led products that:

- ship quickly
- run lean teams
- use AI heavily in planning or implementation
- need tighter connection between product intent, growth learning, and delivery execution

## Best-Fit Customer Traits

- 1 to 10 person team, or solo founder with contractors
- product and engineering decisions currently spread across multiple tools
- roadmap quality depends on one or two people remembering context
- recurring need to transform customer feedback, incidents, market signals, and experiments into structured work
- willingness to adopt a repo-first operating model

## Pain Profile

- unclear prioritization
- stale product docs
- weak connection between customer insight and backlog
- delivery work detached from commercial goals
- too much manual orchestration for AI agents

## High-Value Jobs To Be Done

- help me turn customer and market signals into prioritized work fast
- help me keep strategy, roadmap, and execution aligned
- help me scale my product operating system across multiple products
- help me preserve context so AI agents can work safely and effectively

## Poor-Fit Customers

- large enterprises needing deep governance before value creation
- teams wanting a pure ticketing tool rather than an operating model
- teams unwilling to maintain documentation as a source of truth

# Content Strategy

## Goal

Use content to demonstrate the operating model in practice and attract builders who feel the pain of disconnected startup execution.

## Content Themes

- how to structure a product so AI can ship safely
- startup operating systems for multi-product builders
- product-doc systems that actually survive implementation
- lightweight delivery and SRE patterns for Vercel and AWS products
- turning customer signals into roadmap and task slices

## Asset Types

- worked repo examples
- teardown posts
- implementation notes
- launch checklists
- templates
- before-and-after migration stories

## Content Rule

Prefer practical, reproducible material over abstract thought leadership.

## Conversion Role

Content should:

- attract the right ICP
- prove competence
- demonstrate the shape of the operating system
- make the offer feel concrete and reusable

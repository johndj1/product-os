# Go-To-Market Strategy

## Positioning Hypothesis

Product OS helps founder-led software products run product, delivery, and operations as one coherent system instead of a pile of disconnected tools and prompts.

## Who We Reach First

- solo founders
- tiny startup teams
- technical product builders running multiple products
- AI-native builders who want safer, higher-quality agentic delivery

## Entry Point

Lead with the pain of fragmentation:

- roadmap disconnected from work
- customer feedback disconnected from prioritization
- AI agents lacking grounding
- new projects starting from zero every time

## Adoption Motion

- prove internally across multiple products
- create strong example repos and before/after cases
- publish practical templates and operating-model content
- productize the most reusable flows

## Messaging Pillars

- startup operating system, not just backlog software
- repo-first and AI-readable by design
- connects strategy, growth, engineering, and operations
- designed for real builders shipping under time pressure

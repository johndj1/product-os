# How To Use Personas And Journeys

## Persona Guidance

- Keep personas role-based and operational.
- Tie each persona to WorkItem, Signal, KPI, and Decision responsibilities.
- Avoid generic profiles; focus on product-operating behaviors.

## Journey Guidance

Each journey should include:

- Actor
- Trigger
- Steps
- Pain points
- Opportunities
- Signals
- Desired outcome

## Traceability Rule

Every journey should map to:

- At least one WorkItem that is created or updated.
- At least one Signal source or Signal type.
- At least one KPI it influences.
- Any relevant Decision records.

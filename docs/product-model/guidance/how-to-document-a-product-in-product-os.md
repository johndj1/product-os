# How To Document A Product In Product OS

## Recommended Order

1. Create Product PDD from `product-pdd-template.md`.
2. Create persona files from `persona-template.md`.
3. Create journey files from `customer-journey-template.md`.
4. Define KPI files from `kpi-template.md`.
5. Record major decisions from `decision-template.md`.
6. Capture architecture with `architecture-template.md`.

## Required Mappings Per Product

- WorkItems: primary delivery and strategy nodes.
- Signals: real-world inputs and triggers.
- Relationships: graph traceability across work.
- KPIs: measurable outcomes.
- Decisions: durable decision log.

## Completion Checklist

- Product PDD exists and names primary persona.
- At least one journey includes Signals and desired outcome.
- At least one KPI has baseline and target.
- At least one decision includes consequences and follow-up WorkItems.
